What is EzClass?

EzClass is a class planner for San Diego State students. You search real sections, put together the week you want, and see right away if anything overlaps, all before your registration window opens.

It's built by SDSU students who got tired of planning in a dozen tabs. No sign up, no app to install, and your plans stay on your device.

Key features

Everything you need to plan a semester, on one screen.

Search across subjects

Put CS and MATH in the same list, keep only the days you can be on campus, and hide lower-division courses with one switch.

Preview before you add

Hover over a section and a dashed outline shows where it lands on your week. If it hits something you already have, it turns red.

Auto-build

Give it the courses you need. It tries every combination of sections and ranks the ones that fit by days on campus, gaps, or start time.

Plan around work

Drag across the calendar to block off a shift, practice, or your commute. Classes that run into it get flagged, and Auto-build stays clear.

Compare three plans

Keep a Plan A, B, and C for each term and put them side by side: days on campus, gaps, first class, and overlaps.

Share and export

Send your week to a friend or advisor with one link, or add every class to Google, Apple, or Outlook Calendar as a repeating event.

How to use it

  1. Pick your term. Fall 2026, Spring 2027, or Summer 2027. Each one keeps its own plans.
  2. Find your classes. Search by course, title, or professor, and filter by subject or the days you can be on campus.
  3. Build your week. Hover over a section to preview it, then add it. Or let Auto-build find every schedule that fits.
  4. Check for conflicts. One click confirms nothing overlaps, including your work or practice hours.
  5. Take it with you. Copy a share link or add your classes to your calendar, then register with confidence.

        Questions people ask

        Is this the official SDSU class schedule?

        No. EzClass reads a copy of the class schedule data that we load into our database. Seats and times can change, so check every section in the official SDSU class schedule before you register.

        Does it cost anything?

        No. EzClass is free for students.

        Do I need an account?

        No. Your plans save in this browser. If you clear your browser data they go with it, so copy a share link if you want a backup.

        How do I send my schedule to a friend or advisor?

        Press "Copy share link". The link holds your term and your sections, so whoever opens it sees the same week you do. Nothing gets uploaded.

        What's a tight walk?

        Two classes on the same day, in different buildings, with 15 minutes or less between them. You can make it. You just won't be stopping for coffee.

        Where do the professor ratings come from?

        We don't store any. Clicking a professor's name opens a RateMyProfessors search for them in a new tab.

        Can I plan more than one version of my week?

        Yes. Each term has room for Plan A, B, and C. "Compare plans" puts them side by side so you can see which one has fewer days on campus or shorter gaps.

        Our story

        EzClass started with a problem every one of us kept running into. When registration opened, we'd have the class search in one tab, professor reviews in another, a notes app full of meeting times, and a group chat asking which sections were still open. And almost every semester, someone found out too late that two of their classes overlapped.

        In fall 2026, six of us at San Diego State decided to fix it. We met in a database course, and instead of building something just to turn in, we built the tool we wished we'd had: one place to search sections, see your week, and catch conflicts before they cost you a seat.

        We're still students. We plan around jobs, long commutes, and classes that only offer one section, the same as everyone else. That's the test we hold every feature to: would it have saved us time last semester? If the answer is no, it doesn't ship.

        Privacy

        Last updated October 30, 2026

        The short version

        EzClass has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or student ID, and we don't track you, show ads, or sell anything.

        What stays on your device

        Your plans, busy times, and theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

        What our server sees

        When you search, your browser asks our server for sections that match your filters, such as the term, subject, professor, or a keyword. When you check for conflicts, it sends the section numbers in your plan. Like most web servers, ours may keep basic request logs, which include your IP address and the time of the request.

        Share links

        A share link contains your term and the section numbers in your plan, nothing else. Anyone who has the link can see those classes, so only send it to people you want to see your schedule.

        Other services

        The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. Professor names link to RateMyProfessors, which has its own privacy policy. If you use the version hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement.

        Cookies

        EzClass doesn't set cookies.

        Changes and questions

        If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions can go to us as an issue on our GitHub page.
